# Ricardo And Chats Story Mode

This is the self-contained handoff folder for the Astra League story campaign. It includes copies of the campaign documents, level blockouts, and current concept art. The folder mirrors the repo's `docs/` and `art/` paths so relative references in the copied files continue to work. The original files remain in the repo root, `docs/`, and `art/`.

## Start here

1. Read `HISTORY_MODE.md` for the current expanded campaign order.
2. Read `docs/campaign-level-1-layout.md` and `docs/olocuilta-market-map-guide.md` for Level 1.
3. Read the three sewer design docs in order: `docs/sewer-level-section-01.md`, `docs/sewer-level-primary-canal.md`, and `docs/sewer-level-chase.md`.
4. Open the matching blockouts and concept art in `art/markets/` and `art/sewers/`.
5. Use `art/character-select-transparent/character-select-menu.png` as the reference for the established roster designs and accent colors.

## Current campaign order

- **Level 1 — Olocuilta Market:** rescue civilians, reunite with the squad at the Ferris wheel, hold the distant barricade line, and evacuate as the market burns.
- **Level 2 — The Sewers:** infiltrate toward Hotel Aurora without attracting the city horde. The route begins with an enemy-free movement tutorial, continues into three noisy primary-canal lift defenses with escalating bug swarms, then ends with a giant-beast chase and escape from an outlet three blocks from the hotel.
- **Level 3 — Hotel Aurora:** search for the former squad member's invasion information, survive the ambush, recover the intel, and escape.
- **Level 4 — The Counterattack:** use flying mechs to attack the enemy ship, then fight through the ship and confront the invasion's final boss.

`docs/story-mode-outline.md` is the earlier compact three-level draft, included for its original notes. The sewer sequence was added later and expands the campaign to four levels.

## What's included

- `HISTORY_MODE.md` — updated current story outline.
- `docs/` — level narratives, layouts, and blockout notes.
- `art/` — copies of the repo's art assets, including campaign concepts, maps, enemy/mech/boss references, and roster sheets.

This is a design handoff. The blockouts are conceptual and are not measured architectural plans.
