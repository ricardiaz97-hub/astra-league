# Sewer Level — Primary Canal Encounter

After the enemy-free entry, the squad reaches the enormous primary canal. A large nonhuman shape is glimpsed through spray or behind a maintenance grate across the water; it is a distant sighting, not a boss fight yet. To cross, the team must activate three noisy lift/pump stations. Each raises a canal platform with the water level and opens the next stretch of route. The grinding machinery draws a different bug swarm each time, forcing the squad to hold the station until its lift locks.

## Encounter loop

**Activate lift → machinery roars → defend the station → platform reaches lock height → cross to next bank.** Repeat exactly three times, with a short traversal/combat breather between stations.

1. **Lift 1 — teach:** one small, fast swarm enters from the nearby side culvert. The player sees the pump, hears it start, and learns to defend the activation point. The first lift rises into a clear crossing.
2. **Lift 2 — change the attack angle:** a longer hold. Bugs come from the opposite bank and drop from visible pipework, teaching the player to watch both levels.
3. **Lift 3 — escalate:** largest swarm, mixing fast bugs with one heavier sewer bug. The distant canal creature stirs or strikes the water beyond a grate, then disappears upstream; it is foreshadowing, not a boss arena. The final platform locks and opens the route into the rusty section.

## Blockout rules

- Use a single forward route that alternates banks across three unmistakable lift crossings; avoid maze branches.
- Keep the next station visible from the current crossing where possible.
- Each lift is a large, readable moving platform/sluice gate that rises in stages with the water and forms a safe crossing when locked.
- Pump stations are on dry widened landings, with simple cover and room to fight; don't force players to stand in the water during each hold.
- Make pump noise and enemy source direction legible. Use side culverts for wave 1, high pipes/opposite bank for wave 2, and mixed sources for wave 3.
- The creature remains across the canal and partially obscured. Do not start the full chase until later in the level.
- No timed failure for the holds; waves are the pressure, and the squad can recover between stations.
- This is a gameplay graybox, not a measured infrastructure plan.
