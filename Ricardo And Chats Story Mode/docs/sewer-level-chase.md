# Sewer Chase — Third Lift to the Outlet

This is the escape sequence after the third primary-canal defense. The squad is not asked to defeat the creature. When the canal water surges and the beast breaks through behind them, the objective changes to **RUN** and the player follows one clear route to a sewer outlet three city blocks from Hotel Aurora.

## Chase route

1. **Trigger:** water drains toward the sluice, then surges back. The creature bursts through the gate behind the squad. The warm outlet light is visible ahead.
2. **Sprint and vault:** follow the maintenance ledge, vault a low pipe, and cross the first damaged walkway while the beast smashes the route behind the player.
3. **Wall-run:** use a clearly marked intact wall beside a broken ledge to cross the short collapsed span above the water.
4. **Wall-jump:** jump to the broad, safe landing on the opposite bank. The beast crashes into the gap behind, closing the route but not causing an unavoidable hit.
5. **Final sprint and escape:** vault small debris, reach the fixed ladder, climb through the outlet, and emerge three blocks from Hotel Aurora.

## Blockout rules

- Keep the chase continuous and one-way: no combat arenas, loot detours, forks, or backtracking.
- The beast stays behind the squad and is communicated with splashes, impacts, debris, shadow, and brief clear glimpses.
- Always make the outlet or its warm light the forward landmark.
- Use one low vault, one wall-run, one wall-jump, then a short clear sprint to the ladder.
- Give the player enough runway and readable traversal markings. A missed jump should allow a quick recovery or nearby checkpoint, not an arbitrary instant death.
- Collapse set pieces behind or beside the player so they add urgency without obscuring the route.
- Keep this as a compact authored chase segment at the end of the sewer level.
