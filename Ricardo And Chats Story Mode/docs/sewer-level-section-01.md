# Sewer Section 01 — Entry to the Primary Canal

A calm, enemy-free movement tutorial that links the surface entrance to the huge primary canal. This is one clear route, not a maze. The open manhole remains behind the squad, casting warm daylight down the entry passage; ankle-deep water and a raised maintenance path lead toward the dark canal reveal.

## Route beats

1. Descend the fixed ladder and enter shallow water; show the bright manhole behind the squad as a clear orientation landmark.
2. Follow the raised walkway and vault one low pipe crossing the path.
3. Climb a modest step onto a dry maintenance ledge. A visible stepping-block shortcut crosses the water but rejoins the main route immediately; it is optional, obvious, and contains a small loot pickup.
4. Hop a narrow runoff trench, with a broad safe landing and no punishment for a missed jump.
5. Pass through the arch and reveal the primary canal shown in `olocuilta-sewer-squad-scale-concept.png`.

## Blockout constraints

- No enemies, traps, steam hazards, or combat in this opening section.
- Keep the whole route visible or readable from landmarks; no intersections or maze-like branches.
- Traversal is introductory: one vault, one small step-up, one easy gap hop.
- Maintain shallow water and a parallel raised walkway so the player can read the route.
- Use the descending daylight behind and cooler canal lighting ahead to communicate forward direction.
- This graybox is illustrative, not a measured engineering plan.
