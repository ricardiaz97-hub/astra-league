## Roof clarification

The aerial-front concept includes a partial roof cutaway/opening **only to illustrate what the market interior could look like**. It is not a real opening in the roof and is not meant to change the market's exterior roof design. The real-world reference has a continuous curved blue metal roof.

The ramp location in the concept is provisional until confirmed against a reference showing its actual side.

- `olocuilta-market-interior-wide-angle-concept.png` — wide-angle eye-level view showing the ground-floor aisles, vendor stalls, upper gallery, and ramp. It is a visual layout guide, not a measured reconstruction.

- `olocuilta-market-ground-floor-top-down-concept.png` — illustrated roof-off overhead layout. Cathedral/front is at the bottom; Ferris wheel/back is at the top. The roof is removed only to show the ground-floor stall rows, main aisle, and ramp footprint. The civilian evacuation route continues through the rear doors to the Ferris wheel. This is a conceptual blockout, not a measured plan.

- `olocuilta-market-second-floor-top-down-concept.png` — illustrated roof-off plan of the partial upper gallery. It shows the central atrium, provisional ramp landing, collapsed gallery gap, and wall-run/wall-jump crossing. Conceptual, not measured.

## Level 1 route notes

- Survivors are surrounded by regular invaders in the front-middle ground-floor stalls.
- Ranged mobs hang from and crawl along the ceiling beams.
- Knocked-over stalls teach vaulting on the ground-floor route.
- Evacuation and squad rendezvous are at the Ferris wheel behind the market.
- On the second-floor plan, keep the ramp intact at the lower-right/front end. Put the collapsed wall-run/wall-jump section far toward the north/rear, after a long intact gallery; never place the break on the ramp or landing.

- `olocuilta-ferris-wheel-evac-zone-top-down-concept.png` — large rear fairground defense/extraction area. It leaves roughly 200 meters of open approach between the market's rear doors and the distant squad barricades, with separated cover islands so hordes can be seen advancing. Civilians pass through a defended gap to cover behind the squad; the evac ship landing zone sits near the Ferris wheel.
