# Sewer concept art

- `olocuilta-sewer-squad-scale-concept.png` — wide establishing view of the squad entering the sewer route toward Hotel Aurora. The tiny characters, repeated monumental arches, oversized drainage pipes, high galleries, and central water channel communicate the sewer's scale. This is atmosphere and scale guidance; it is not a gameplay map or measured infrastructure plan.

- `olocuilta-sewer-beast-chase-concept.png` — the roster runs through the same sewer as a gigantic bug beast charges behind them; the traversal props and outlet light indicate the escape direction. Concept art, not a gameplay map.

- `sewer-section-01-entry-graybox.svg` — top-down plan plus side elevation for the enemy-free entry tutorial: ladder, ankle-deep water, one vault, one step-up, optional short loot shortcut, easy gap hop, and reveal of the primary canal.

- `primary-canal-three-lift-graybox.svg` — three readable canal crossings with a pump defense at each; noise draws escalating bug swarms. The distant creature is foreshadowed but not fought here.
