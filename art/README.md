# Astra League character art

Player character sheets, T-poses, and selection art are in the sibling folders. Enemy concepts are in `mobs/`.

- `mobs/regular-mob.png`: standard non-human insectoid enemy creature with a compact, balanced silhouette and violet details.
- `mobs/ranged-mob-skinny.png`: lanky ranged variant from the same creature family, with a tail-mounted crystal shard launcher.
- `mobs/huge-brawler-mob.png`: oversized, slightly humanoid-in-stance bruiser creature with heavy chitin hammer-claws.
