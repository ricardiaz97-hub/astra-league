# Olocuilta Market — Campaign Map Visual Guide

## Orientation and real-world references

- **Front / south:** cathedral side, columned public entrance, and forecourt.
- **Back / north:** fairground courtyard with the Ferris wheel. This is the evacuation zone and squad rendezvous.
- The market is large and has two stories.
- The main ramp is on the east/right side in the current concept. Its exact real-world side still needs confirmation.
- The illustrated plans are relative blockouts, not measured architectural drawings.
- The aerial concept's roof cutaway is only to show the interior; the real market roof is intact.

**Keep the ramp and broken crossing separate.** The ramp is at the lower-right/front end and must remain intact. The collapsed crossing is much farther north at the back of the gallery, after a long stretch of safe walkway. The wall-run surface and wall-jump landing should be clearly visible from the approach.

## Enemy and tutorial beats

1. **Survivor rescue:** regular mobs surround survivors among the front-middle market stalls. Defeating or displacing them opens the central route.
2. **Vaulting:** knocked-over stalls teach the vault-over mechanic. Use one low, clear obstacle first, followed by a small number of varied stalls.
3. **Ceiling threat:** ranged mobs hang from ceiling beams and crawl above the market aisle. Telegraph their shots and silhouettes so players can track the overhead threat.
4. **Upper-floor traversal:** the player goes up the ramp, follows the intact gallery, then uses wall-run and wall-jump to cross a collapsed section near the back.
5. **Rendezvous and hold:** survivors reach the rear fairground; the squad is holding a distant barricade line, and calls the player over. The ship is five minutes away, so the squad must protect the civilians until then.

## Rear fairground — long approach and evacuation defense

The fairground is the biggest combat space in the level. Leave roughly **200 meters of open ground** between the market's rear doors and the squad's barricade line. Treat this as a target for game scale, not an exact survey measurement.

```text
                                 NORTH / FERRIS WHEEL
                  [covered civilian holding areas] [covered areas]
                            [SHIP LANDING ZONE]
                         behind / north of the squad

                SQUAD POSITIONS — DEFEND THIS LINE
          ═══ barricade ═══   CIVILIAN GATE   ═══ barricade ═══
                             ↑ civilians pass behind the squad
                             ↑ to perimeter cover and ship

             open fairground approach, approx. 200 m
       sparse cover islands: planters, carts, benches, stalls
       leave long sightlines so hordes can be seen advancing

                    rear market doors / burning market
                                 SOUTH
```

### Defense sequence

1. When the player exits the market, show the distant squad behind barricades and give a clear objective marker: **reach the squad**.
2. As the player crosses the long plaza, hordes emerge from the rear market doors and both side streets. Spaced cover islands create a dangerous but readable advance.
3. At the barricades, a teammate calls the player over and says the evac ship will arrive in five minutes.
4. The squad holds the market-facing line while civilians pass through the controlled gap and move behind the defenders into cover on the north/east and north/west perimeter.
5. Defend against escalating waves for the full five-minute timer. Keep the central landing zone and civilian route clear; enemies should attack the line from the south and side streets.
6. The evac ship lands in the open area beyond the squad near the Ferris wheel. Stop new waves, board the squad and civilians, and depart while seeing the market burn behind them.

Keep the barricade line far from the market exit; do not place it at the rear doors. The open approach should feel much larger than the market interior, with a few cover islands rather than a continuous wall of cover.
